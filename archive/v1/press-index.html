02 / BOILERPLATE

The short version. The long version.

Copy, paste, trim. Use either one as-is.

Short about 50 words

Dumpster is a chat-first app that replaces texting yourself. Dump links, addresses, photos, voice notes and half-thoughts into one thread. Scraps, a trash-panda mascot, sorts everything into Piles, and you Dig it back up in plain language. Zero effort in, organized out. Coming soon to iOS and Android.

Long about 200 words

Most of us already use a thread with ourselves as a notebook. A link here, an address there, a screenshot we meant to read. It works for about a day, and then it turns into a graveyard no one can search by memory.

Dumpster keeps the part that works, which is the thread, and fixes the part that doesn't. You dump anything into one chat: links, addresses, photos, screenshots, voice notes, half-finished thoughts. Each one becomes a smart card, so a link looks like a link and an address looks like a place. Quietly, Dumpster files it into a Pile (Places, Watch Later, Reads, Shopping, To-dos and more) without asking you to name a folder or pick a tag.

When you need something back, you Dig. Ask in plain language, like "that address mom sent", and Scraps, the raccoon who lives in the app, comes back with the answer and the original. Now and then it will also nudge you with a "Remember this?" about something you saved and forgot. You can share to Dumpster from anywhere on your phone, too.

It is built on one idea: people won't organize, but they will dump. So Dumpster does the organizing.

Dumpster is coming soon to iOS and Android. It was designed by Range Of View Studios.

04 / KEY FEATURES

Six things worth knowing

One thread for everything

Links, addresses, photos, screenshots, voice notes and half-thoughts all go in the same place. No folders to pick, no app to choose.

Smart cards

A link shows up as a preview, an address as a place with a map button, a to-do as a checkbox. What you dump looks like what it is.

Auto Piles

Everything is quietly filed into Places, Watch Later, Reads, Shopping, People, To-dos and Ideas. Wrong pile? One tap moves it.

Dig in plain language

Ask the way you would ask a friend: "that ramen place Jay sent". Scraps answers and shows you the original.

Remember this?

Once a day, a card from the past: the video you never watched, the idea you almost had. Useful, and never naggy.

Share from anywhere

Dumpster sits in your phone's share sheet. See something in any app, tap share, and it is tossed in the right Pile.
