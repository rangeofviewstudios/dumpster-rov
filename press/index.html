02 · Boilerplate

Words you can use

Lift them as they are, or trim to fit.

Short · about 50 words

Dumpster is a chat app that replaces texting yourself. Dump links, addresses, photos, voice notes and half-thoughts into one thread. It files them into Piles, and you Dig them back up in plain language. No folders, no tags. Zero effort in, organized out. Coming soon to iOS and Android.

Long · about 200 words

Most of us already keep a thread with ourselves. A link, an address, a screenshot we meant to read. It works for about a day, then it becomes a place nothing comes back from.

Dumpster keeps the thread and fixes the rest. You dump anything into one chat: links, addresses, photos, screenshots, voice notes, half-finished thoughts. Each one becomes a card that looks like what it is, a link as a link, an address as a place. Then Dumpster files it into a Pile (Places, Watch Later, Reads, Shopping, To-dos and more) without asking you to name a folder or pick a tag.

Sending is the one moment of ceremony. Every message you send drops into the dumpster: the lid lifts, the message falls in, the lid shuts, and the word FILED appears. It takes about a second and it is the only animation in the app that hurries.

When you need something back, you Dig. Ask in plain language, "that address mom sent", and Dumpster answers with the original. You can also share to it from anywhere on your phone.

The idea is simple. People won't organize, but they will dump. So Dumpster does the organizing.

Dumpster is coming soon to iOS and Android. It is designed by Range Of View Studios.

03 · The problem, the fix

Mom texts you an address

MomDinner is at 42 Maple Court. Bring the good bread.
You, to you42 Maple Court
three weeks later
Youwhat was that address mom sent

The problem. You forward the address to yourself so you won't lose it. Weeks on, it's under a pile of screenshots and you're scrolling with your thumb and a bad feeling. The usual fix, a notes app with folders, asks for more effort than the problem is worth.

The fix. In Dumpster the address lands as a place card, gets filed in Places, and comes back when you type "that address mom sent". Nobody organized anything. People won't. They'll only dump, so that's all it asks.

04 · The send

Everything you send is filed

The one flourish in an otherwise quiet app.

  1. The lid lifts. The send button is the dumpster, so there is nowhere else to look.
  2. The message drops in. It shrinks and falls. This is the only quick, physical motion in the app.
  3. The lid shuts. A small four-point glint, then FILED → PLACES fades in under the message.

Blue  is the only color in the app, and it only ever marks what you sent. With reduced motion on, the flight becomes a fade.

10 · Usage

Please don't

Recolor the mark. Bone on dark, ink on light. Use the files as supplied.
Use blue for anything but sent messages. It is the one color and it means "you sent this".
Crowd it. Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of its lid.
Put it on busy photos. Use a quiet area or a flat background.
