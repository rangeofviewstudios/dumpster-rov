01 / Reference

What we took from blackstar.inc

The site is almost nothing: a centered wordmark, one list of open roles and a footer. That restraint is the whole point. These are the moves that make it feel expensive.

Near-black, never flat

A black base lit by many faint, silvery radial glows and a film-grain texture. It reads as smoke or a dim studio, not a solid fill.

Monochrome only

Off-white text (#EEEEEE, warm #F4F1EC) at a few opacities. No accent color at all. Hierarchy comes from opacity and size.

A star glyph as the logo

A small four-point sparkle next to a wide, clean uppercase wordmark. The glyph does the brand work; the type stays neutral.

Two quiet typefaces

Space Grotesk for labels and headings, ABC Diatype for text. Micro labels are uppercase, around 11px, tracked very wide (0.32em).

Hairlines instead of boxes

Content sits in lists divided by 1px low-opacity rules. No cards, no shadows, no borders around things.

Huge negative space

Everything is centered in a narrow column with a lot of dark around it. Nothing competes.

Slow, ambient motion

The light layers drift gently. Nothing bounces or squishes.

Voice: big idea, few words

"The bridge between human intention and machine understanding." Short, confident, slightly cosmic.

04 / Color

Monochrome, plus one blue

Six neutral tones and a single color. Blue appears only on what you send, so your own words are the brightest thing on screen. Piles are names, not colors.

05 / Type

Two typefaces, both quiet

Free stand-ins for Blackstar's pairing. Diatype is a paid font; Geist is the closest free match.

Display + labels
Space Grotesk

Headings set light (300–400) and tight. Labels uppercase at 11.5px, tracked 0.32em.

Text + UI
Geist

Message text, card titles and settings. Geist Mono for timestamps, sources and counts.

08 / The shift

From loud to lit

Before · v1
  • Hazard yellow + 8 pile colors
  • Thick black borders, hard shadows
  • Scraps the raccoon mascot
  • Archivo Black, chunky
  • Squishy, bouncy buttons
  • Puns: Toss it, Dig
After · proposed
  • Near-black with grain and soft light; blue only on sent messages
  • 1px hairlines, no shadows
  • A fine-line dumpster mark; Scraps only as an easter egg
  • Space Grotesk + Geist, light weights
  • Slow fades and drifting light
  • Fewer words; dry wit, not puns
09 / Confirmed

What we agreed

Decisions from founder review. These steer the rebuild of every deliverable.

Full switch

Blackstar-level restraint: near-black, grain, soft light, monochrome. One color: blue, used only for messages you send. Piles are names, not colors.

The dumpster is the logo, and the interaction

A fine-line dumpster mark. Every message you send drops into it.

Scraps becomes an easter egg

The raccoon leaves the brand front and shows up only in rare moments, redrawn in fine line.

Dark-first, with a light paper mode

Dark is the brand. A bone "paper" theme is available in settings.

Same product, new skin

The flow (Dump, Piles, Dig), screens and features stay. Brand kit, demo, showcase and press kit get rebuilt in the new system.
