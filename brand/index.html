Brand guidelines for a chat-first app that replaces texting yourself. Quiet, dark, precise. The restraint is the brand.

Brand guidelines / v2.0 · Range Of View Studios · October 2026
01 / The idea

A luxury object with a joke for a name

Dumpster is where everything you send yourself ends up, sorted, and findable in plain language. The name is the joke. The design never repeats it.

Dump

One thread. Links, addresses, screenshots, voice notes, half-thoughts. Zero effort in.

Piles

Dumpster sorts what you sent into Piles. Names, not colors. A plain list, divided by hairlines.

Dig

Ask for it the way you remember it. "That address Mom sent." A quiet answer line, then the cards.

Posture

Quiet. Nocturnal. Precise. Archival. Unbothered. If in doubt, remove something.

Reference

Blackstar's discipline: a tiny glyph beside a wide uppercase wordmark, near-black lit by soft silver light, one list, lots of dark. The only color we add is one blue, reserved for what you send.

02 / Logo

A fine-line dumpster,
and a wide wordmark

The mark is a side view of a front-load dumpster: flat lid, boxy body, one rib, one fork pocket, two casters. One stroke weight, round joins. The lid is its own element, because it opens.

Sizes and stroke weight

200 · 64 · 32 · 24 · 16

Draw the stroke in pixels, not units. Stroke weight is set per size so the line stays fine as the mark grows and never clots as it shrinks. At 24px and below, drop the rib and the fork pocket (mark-small.svg).

Wordmark: DUMPSTER in Space Grotesk 500, tracked 0.064em, with hand-tuned pair spacing (UM −8, MP −10, PS +14, ST +10, TE −8, ER +16 in 1000-unit em). It ships as outlined paths, so no font is needed. Lockup stroke is 1.95u at 1.78× scale, about 3.5px against an 8px wordmark stem: a thin mark beside a firm name, like Blackstar's star.

App icon

1024 · rounded square · void + soft light + grain

Mark centred in bone on void; stroke 1.05u at 10.6× (about 11px at 1024). The light is a soft radial from the top and a faint one at lower right, plus 7% grain. Flat-safe: the stores apply their own mask, so no baked corners are needed beyond the PNG preview. Stroke is not thinned for small icon sizes.

03 / Logo explorations

Five drawings, one kept

The first mark read as a shopping cart. We redrew it from the side, where a front-load dumpster has a silhouette nothing else shares, and tested every version at 16 to 200px on void and paper.

A · Chosen

Side, closed

Boxy body, flat lid proud of it, one rib, one fork pocket, small casters. Holds at 16px, never reads as a cart, and the lid hinges cleanly.

B · Runner-up

Front, split lid

Boxy front with a double lid and two ribs. Close to the old mark: a cabinet or crate at small sizes, and two lids is a messier animation.

C · Runner-up

Three-quarter

Single-weight line, lid as a slab. Lovely large, but the extra planes clot below 32px and it reads as a gift box.

D · Kept as a state

Side, lid ajar + glint

The most dumpster of all, and a nod to Blackstar's star. But a mark that is always open cannot rest, so it became mark-ajar.svg for empty and ready states.

E · Rejected

Side, slanted front

Our first side drawing. A sloped front plus big wheels turned it back into a trolley. Kept here so nobody redraws it.

Rows top to bottom: 200px, 64, 32, 24, 16, each at its own stroke weight.

04 / The send

Everything you send
goes in the dumpster

The one signature interaction. The message flies into the mark, the lid shuts, a glint says it is filed. It is the only fast, physical motion in the brand. Press send, or let it run.

05 / Color

Monochrome, plus one blue

Six neutrals and a single color. Hierarchy comes from opacity and size, not hue. Piles are names, not colors.

07 / Light and texture

Never flat

Near-black lit by faint silver radials, with film grain on top. It should read as smoke in a dim studio, not as a fill.

Void + 3 radials + grain
/* the recipe, from assets/tokens.css */
.bg-void {
  background:
    radial-gradient(circle at 54% 12%, rgba(222,228,233,.06), transparent 42%),
    radial-gradient(circle at 18% 60%, rgba(176,183,189,.035), transparent 40%),
    radial-gradient(circle at 82% 82%, rgba(204,210,214,.03), transparent 45%),
    var(--void);
}
.bg-void::after {
  content: ""; position: absolute; inset: 0;
  background-image: var(--grain);   /* feTurbulence, .85 freq */
  opacity: .07; mix-blend-mode: overlay;
  pointer-events: none;
}
/* drift: 18s ease-in-out, light layers only */
Radials

Three, between 3% and 6% silver. A fourth is allowed in hero areas. Never brighter than 16%, never colored.

Grain

6–8% on dark, overlay blend. Paper mode uses 6% black grain. Grain sits above the light and below the content.

Drift

15–20 seconds, background-position only. It pauses under reduced motion.

Lines

1px hairlines at 12% (--line) or 24% (--line-strong) instead of boxes. No drop shadows, no thick borders, no hard offsets. The only glow in the product is the pin on a map card.

10 / Motion

Slow and ambient.
Quick only when you send.

The product is quiet, so movement is rare and it means something.

Ambient

The light layers drift over 15–20s. Fades and cross-fades run 200–600ms on --ease-out.

One physical moment

The send flight: lid, drop, shut, glint. 260 to 640ms per beat, --ease-drop on the fall. Nothing else flies.

Nothing bounces

No springs, no squish, no overshoot beyond the 3° lid settle. Press states are opacity, not scale.

Easing

--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for arrivals · --ease-drop: cubic-bezier(.55,0,.8,.3) for things falling.

Reduced motion

Honor prefers-reduced-motion: drift stops, the send becomes a fade, the glint is dropped. State still communicates through the filed label.

Focus

1px bone outline, 3px offset, on every interactive element. Always visible with the keyboard.

11 / Voice

Dry wit, few words

Grounded, warm, refined. Say it in as few words as you can, and stop. Lowercase-friendly in the UI, uppercase tracked for micro-labels. The name is the joke; we don't make more.

Rules

No exclamation marks. No emoji. No puns on the product name. No "AI-powered". Wit is dry and rare, one idea per screen at most.

Test

Read it aloud. If it sounds like a LinkedIn post or a 3am infomercial, rewrite it shorter.

12 / Scraps

Scraps, retired.
Almost.

The raccoon left the brand front. He survives as a rare fine-line easter egg, drawn in the same 1.5px stroke language as the icons.

scraps-fineline.svg
Where

An empty Dig result. A long-press on the logo. The 404. Maybe an About screen.

How often

Rare. Never twice in one session, never on a first run.

Never

In marketing, in the app icon, in the nav. Never colored, never filled, never animated with bounce. No speech bubbles, no puns.

Pose

Peeking over a rim line, paws on the edge. He does not leave the frame.

Line

1.5px stroke at 96px grid, round caps, currentColor. At 24px he is not drawn.

Copy

"Nothing under that." Just that. Scraps doesn't talk.
